Pads, tampons, menstrual cups, and menstrual discs are all commonly used options, and there's no
single "best" choice — what works well often comes down to personal comfort and your flow.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Whichever internal product you use (tampons, cups, or discs), follow the
wear-time guidance on the packaging and wash your hands before and after use — this lowers the
already-rare risk of toxic shock syndrome (TSS), a serious infection. Watch for sudden fever, rash,
vomiting, or feeling faint while using any internal product, and remove it and seek care right away if
that happens.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] It's fine to try different products, including switching between them
across a cycle depending on your flow, and reusable options (cups, discs, washable pads) can be
cost-effective over time. If a product is consistently uncomfortable or hard to use, that's worth
mentioning to a clinician rather than just living with it.
