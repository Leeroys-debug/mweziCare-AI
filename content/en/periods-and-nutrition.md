[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Because you lose some blood during your period, it's a good time to pay
attention to iron — low iron over time can contribute to tiredness. Iron-rich foods include red meat,
fish, lentils, beans, spinach and other leafy greens, and fortified cereals. Pairing plant-based
(non-meat) iron sources with vitamin C — like citrus fruit, tomatoes, or peppers — can help your body
absorb more of it.

Eating regularly and staying hydrated can help with energy levels during your period. Some people also
find foods rich in magnesium — nuts, seeds, whole grains, leafy greens — helpful for cramping, though
this varies from person to person.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] If you have consistently heavy periods and often feel very tired, weak, or
short of breath, diet alone may not be enough — it's worth asking a clinician about checking your iron
levels.
