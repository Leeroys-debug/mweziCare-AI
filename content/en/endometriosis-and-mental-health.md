[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Living with ongoing pain can affect more than your body — research
consistently finds higher rates of anxiety and low mood among people with endometriosis and other
chronic pelvic pain conditions. This isn't a personal weakness; persistent pain genuinely affects mood,
and low mood can make pain feel worse too, so the two are closely connected.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Feeling frustrated, isolated, or unheard — especially if it's taken a long
time to be believed or diagnosed — is a common and understandable reaction, not an overreaction. Talking
to someone you trust, whether that's a friend, family member, or counsellor, can help, and it's a
reasonable thing to raise with a clinician alongside your physical symptoms.

If you're ever having thoughts of harming yourself, please reach out for support right away — the Ask
tab in this app is built to respond safely if you mention that, and the Care tab can help connect you
with a clinician.
