[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] A missed period has many possible explanations. If you're sexually active,
pregnancy is the first thing to rule out with a test. Outside of pregnancy, common causes include
significant stress, big changes in weight, intense exercise, hormonal contraception, and simply having
naturally irregular cycles, especially in the first couple of years after periods start.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Missing periods for three months or more in a row, or having fewer than
nine periods in a year, is worth discussing with a clinician — there can be hormonal reasons behind it
that are worth checking.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] If you're 15 or older and have never had a period, or 13 or older with no
other signs of puberty, that's also worth a clinician visit. This app can't tell you why your period
is missing — tracking what's been happening around it (stress, exercise, weight, any symptoms) is the
most useful thing you can bring to that conversation.
