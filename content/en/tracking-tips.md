Tracking regularly — even on light or "nothing to report" days — gives the clearest picture of your
cycle over time. A few tips: log on the same day you notice something rather than trying to remember
later; use the pain and symptom options consistently so your own patterns are easy to compare cycle to
cycle; and note impact (missed school, work, sleep, or activities), since that's often more useful than
a pain number alone.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] If you're ever unsure whether to answer a safety-check question "yes," it's
better to answer honestly than to guess — it only affects whether you're shown urgent-care guidance,
never anything else.

You can download a summary of your tracked data to share with a clinician whenever you choose to from
the Summary page.
