[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] It sounds like you might be going through something really difficult right
now. You deserve support, and you don't have to face this alone.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] If you're in immediate danger, please contact emergency services or go to
the nearest hospital now. If you can, reach out to a trusted adult, counsellor, or helpline to talk
about how you're feeling.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] This app can't provide crisis counselling, but the Care tab can help you
find a nearby facility, and a clinician or counsellor can connect you with the right support.
