[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] "Period pain is just something you have to live with." Mild cramping is
common, but pain that disrupts your life isn't something to simply endure — it's worth tracking and
discussing with a clinician.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] "You can't get pregnant during your period." Pregnancy risk is lower but not
zero at any point in the cycle for people who are sexually active — this app doesn't give contraception
guidance, but a clinician can.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] "Irregular cycles always mean something is wrong." Cycle length can vary
quite a bit, especially in the first few years after periods start. Persistent irregularity is still
worth tracking and mentioning to a clinician, but it isn't automatically a sign of a problem.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] "Talking about periods is shameful or embarrassing." Periods are a normal
part of life for most girls and women — understanding your own body helps you get care sooner when you
need it.
