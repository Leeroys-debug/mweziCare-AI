[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Bleeding is often considered heavy when you're soaking through a pad or
tampon faster than about every 1-2 hours, passing large clots, or needing to change protection
overnight. Periods lasting longer than about 7 days can also be worth a closer look.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Heavy or prolonged bleeding over several cycles, or bleeding between
periods, is a pattern worth discussing with a clinician — it can have several possible causes, and a
clinician is best placed to work out what's going on for you.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] If heavy bleeding comes with dizziness, fainting, or feeling like you might
pass out, treat that as urgent — seek care right away rather than waiting.
