For most people, moderate exercise during your period is safe, and some find it actually helps with
cramps and mood — movement can ease tension and lift energy, even when the last thing you feel like
doing is working out.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Listen to your body: if you feel dizzy, unusually weak, or are in
significant pain, it's fine to switch to something lighter — gentle stretching or walking — or to rest
instead. Heavy flow can also make high-intensity or contact activity less comfortable; choosing
products suited to your flow and having a backup plan can help.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] There's no single "right" amount of exercise during your period — it depends
on your own flow, energy, and how you feel that day. If pain during exercise is severe or stops you
taking part in things you normally would, that's worth mentioning to a clinician.
