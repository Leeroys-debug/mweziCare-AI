[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] "Pregnancy cures endometriosis." Pregnancy can ease symptoms temporarily for
some people — mainly because periods pause — but it doesn't remove the tissue causing the condition,
and symptoms often return afterward.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] "A hysterectomy always cures it." Removing the uterus addresses only part of
the picture, since endometriosis tissue is often found elsewhere in the pelvis too. Some people feel
much better afterward; others continue to have pain, so this isn't a guaranteed fix.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] "It's just bad period pain." Endometriosis pain can go well beyond typical
cramps — it can happen outside your period, during bowel movements, urination, or sex, and can
seriously disrupt daily life. Dismissing it as "just" period pain is a big reason diagnosis often takes
years.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] "If you have endometriosis, you definitely can't get pregnant." Many people
with endometriosis do conceive, sometimes without any extra help — see "Endometriosis and fertility"
for more.
