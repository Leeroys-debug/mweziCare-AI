Good menstrual hygiene is simple: change your pad, tampon, cup, or disc regularly (following the
guidance for whichever product you use), wash your hands before and after changing it, and wash the
outer genital area with water — and mild, unscented soap if you use one — during your bath or shower.
The vagina cleans itself internally and doesn't need washing inside.

Having clean water, privacy, and somewhere to dispose of used products safely makes managing your
period much easier. If you don't have reliable access to these at school or work, you're not alone —
it's worth raising with a trusted adult or teacher, or using the Care tab if you need help finding
support.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Unusual odour, itching, or irritation that doesn't go away with normal
hygiene is worth mentioning to a clinician — it isn't something you need to just manage on your own.
