[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Periods look different for everyone, and "normal" covers a wide range.
Many young people have cycles anywhere from about 21 to 35 days apart, with bleeding lasting around
2 to 7 days. Some cramping, mild fatigue, or mood changes in the days before or during your period are
common.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Your own normal can also change over the first few years after your period
starts, as cycles become more regular. Tracking your cycle over time — not comparing to a single
"textbook" number — is the best way to learn what's typical for you.

This is general information, not a diagnosis. If something feels different or worrying for you, the
Track and Ask tabs can help you notice patterns, and the Care tab can connect you with a clinician.
