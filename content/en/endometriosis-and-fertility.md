[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Most people with endometriosis are able to become pregnant. It can make
conceiving harder for some — estimates suggest roughly a third of people with endometriosis experience
difficulty — but having endometriosis does not mean you can't have children.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] If you have endometriosis and are trying to conceive, it's reasonable to see
a clinician sooner rather than waiting — a diagnosis mainly changes the timing of when to seek a
fertility evaluation, not whether pregnancy is possible. Many people find their symptoms ease during
pregnancy, though pregnancy itself doesn't treat or cure the condition.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] This is general information, not a prediction about your own fertility — a
gynaecologist can assess your individual situation and discuss options with you.
