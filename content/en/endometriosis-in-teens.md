[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Endometriosis often starts during the teenage years, not just in adulthood —
but it's frequently missed at this age because severe period pain gets assumed to be "normal" and
something to just push through. Research suggests young people can wait a long time for a diagnosis,
partly because symptoms can look similar to other causes of pelvic pain.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Pain from endometriosis in teens isn't necessarily milder than in adults —
some studies suggest it can be just as intense, or more so. If period pain is severe, doesn't respond
to usual self-care, happens outside your period, or regularly makes you miss school, it's worth having
a clinician look into it rather than assuming it's something you'll simply grow out of.

Tracking your symptoms over time — what hurts, when, and how it affects your day — gives a clinician
real information to work with, and this app's Track and Summary tabs can help with exactly that.
