[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Mild to moderate cramping during your period is common and often
manageable at home. Things some people find helpful include a warm compress or hot water bottle on
the lower abdomen or back, gentle movement or stretching, staying hydrated, and resting when you can.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Over-the-counter pain relief can help some people, but this app does not
give medication names, doses, or timing — a pharmacist or clinician can advise on what's appropriate
for you.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Self-care tips are not a substitute for care when pain is severe, sudden,
or stopping you from daily life — see "When pain needs attention" for signs it's worth seeing a
clinician sooner.
