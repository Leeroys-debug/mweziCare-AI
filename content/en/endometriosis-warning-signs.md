[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Endometriosis is a condition where tissue similar to the lining of the
womb grows elsewhere in the body. It can cause pain patterns like severe period cramps, pain between
periods, pain with bowel movements or urination during your period, or pain during sex — but having
one or more of these does not mean you have it, and only a clinician can assess and diagnose this.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] This app never tells you that you have endometriosis or any other
condition. What it can do is help you notice and describe patterns over time — repeated disruption to
school, work, or sleep; worsening cramps; or pain outside your period — so a clinician has useful
information to work with when you see them.

If these patterns sound familiar, consider using the Care tab to find a clinician who can properly
assess you.
