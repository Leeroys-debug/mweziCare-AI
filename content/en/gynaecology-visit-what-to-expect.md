[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] A gynaecology visit usually starts with the clinician asking about your
periods, symptoms, and general health — your tracked logs or a clinician summary from this app can
help you answer these questions. Depending on why you're there, they may also do a physical
examination; they should explain what they're doing and why, and you can ask questions at any point.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] You're allowed to bring someone with you for support, ask for a different
clinician if you're not comfortable, and decline any part of an examination. There's no "wrong"
question to ask.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] After the visit, you might get a plan for self-care, a referral for tests,
or a follow-up appointment — what happens next depends on your individual situation.
