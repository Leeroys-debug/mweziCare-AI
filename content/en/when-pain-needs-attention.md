[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Most period pain is manageable with self-care, but some patterns are worth
having a clinician look at sooner rather than later — for example, pain that keeps you from school,
work, sleep, or your usual activities more than once in a while; pain that's been getting worse over
recent cycles; or pain on days outside your period.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Sudden, severe pain, fainting, or pain alongside a possible pregnancy are
different — those need urgent care right away, not a wait-and-see approach. The Track tab's safety
check and the Care tab can help you act quickly if that's what's happening.

This page describes patterns to notice, not a diagnosis. Tracking in this app can help you and a
clinician see what's been happening over time.
