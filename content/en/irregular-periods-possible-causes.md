[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Cycle length naturally varies, and many things can shift it temporarily —
stress, travel, significant weight change in either direction, intense exercise, illness, and hormonal
contraception are all common causes of irregular periods.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Ongoing irregularity can sometimes point to an underlying hormonal pattern,
such as polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) or a thyroid condition — but having irregular periods doesn't
mean you have either of these, and only a clinician can work out the cause through a conversation and,
if needed, some tests.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] It's worth mentioning irregular periods to a clinician if they've lasted
several cycles, if periods last longer than about 7 days, or if irregularity comes with other changes
like unusual hair growth, persistent tiredness, or difficulty trying to conceive. Tracking your cycles
in this app gives you a clear record to share.
