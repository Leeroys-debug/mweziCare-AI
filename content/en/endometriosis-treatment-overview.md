[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] There's no cure for endometriosis, but there are ways to manage the pain and
other symptoms it causes. This app doesn't recommend specific medications, doses, or brands — a
clinician will tailor treatment to your situation — but it's useful to know the broad categories
clinicians typically consider.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Pain-relief medication is often tried first. Hormonal treatments (such as
certain contraceptive methods) are commonly used next, aiming to reduce or stop periods and slow the
growth of endometriosis tissue — these can ease symptoms but don't remove the underlying tissue, and
symptoms can return once treatment stops. Surgery to remove endometriosis tissue is considered when
other approaches aren't enough, particularly for more severe cases.

[CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Pelvic floor physiotherapy and other supportive approaches are sometimes
used alongside medical treatment. The right combination depends on symptom severity, your age, whether
you're hoping to become pregnant, and your own preferences — this is a decision to make together with
a gynaecologist, not something to self-manage.
